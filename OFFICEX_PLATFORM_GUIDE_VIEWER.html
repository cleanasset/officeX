Page 19: Rent Roll Master Engine

Portal: Landlord Portfolio • Roles: CFOs, Leasing Directors, Property Accountants
/properties/rent-roll
🎯 1. Primary Purpose & Business Value

Institutional-grade Rent Roll engine governing all lease financials, monthly billing schedules, contractual escalations, and lock-in covenants.

🔄 2. Step-by-Step User Flow
  1. Billing Cycle: Selects Property and Billing Cycle.
  2. Multi-Tab Navigation:
    • Rent Roll Grid: Tenant, Unit, Leased Sq.Ft., Lease Dates, Base Rent, CAM, Utilities, GST, Total Billing.
    • Invoices Tab: Approves and bulk-dispatches monthly tax invoices.
    • Collections & Aging: Monitors paid vs. unpaid dues.
    • Escalation Schedule: 12-month forward look of upcoming rent escalations.
    • P&L / NOI: Net Operating Income calculation.

Page 26: Kanban Helpdesk Command Centre

Portal: FM Operations • Roles: Helpdesk Coordinators, Facility Executives, Technicians
/ops/helpdesk
🎯 1. Primary Purpose & Business Value

Drag-and-drop Kanban service desk for triaging, dispatching, and resolving facility incidents with real-time dynamic SLA countdown timers.

🔄 2. Step-by-Step User Flow
  1. Kanban Columns: Tickets organized across *New*, *Assigned*, *In Progress*, and *Resolved*.
  2. Dispatch: Drag ticket to *Assigned*, choose technician or vendor, and alert their mobile app.
  3. Resolution: Technician attaches photo proof; FM moves ticket to *Resolved*.

Page 72: Razorpay Nodal Escrow Controller

Portal: Super Admin • Roles: Financial Controllers, Escrow Officers, Super Admins
/admin/escrow
🎯 1. Primary Purpose & Business Value

Central ledger for governing funds held in Razorpay Nodal Escrow accounts, managing split disbursements, and resolving billing disputes.

🔄 2. Step-by-Step User Flow
  1. Ledger Audit: Reviews Total Escrow Held, Released Funds, Retained Commission (10%), and Disputed Milestones.
  2. Disbursement: Clicks *Release* to disburse funds to vendor bank accounts upon FM signoff.
  3. Dispute Arbitration: Investigates client/vendor evidence for contested milestones.
