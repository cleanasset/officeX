=== SECTION at index 3559: Scalezix Development Rules ===
Scalezix Development Rules
Non-negotiable; merged from both earlier documents
Standalone product, single platform. Build Rent Roll on the OFFICEX Platform Core. Do not create a separate application, database or code base — and do not make any Rent Roll function depend on CAFM, Leasing CRM or Marketplace data.
Do not create duplicate property, space, occupant or user masters.
Enforce entitlements server-side; never rely on hiding UI.
Do not hard-code charge types, tax rates, escalation rules, billing models, numbering or segment behaviour — use configuration.
Separate contractual terms from monthly calculated transactions.
Separate invoices from payments and payment allocation.
Every row carries org_id and client_account_id; every query respects RLS.
Never overwrite executed documents or issued invoices/statements; use versions and adjustment notes.
Every financial and contractual change must be auditable.
Money and area as numeric, never float; all invoice/payment writes idempotent.
Configuration-driven workflows and permissions; API-first for accounting, e-sign, CAFM, CRM, Marketplace and future Intelligence.
Never trust client-supplied organisation, client-account or property IDs; authorise every request server-side.
Preserve unmapped source columns in staging; source-specific columns never become production schema.
A contract is entered once; documents, charges, billing, collections, occupancy, forecast, expiry, P&L and reporting all flow from that one controlled record.
UI must support drill-down portfolio → client → property → building → space → occupant → contract → invoice → payment.

VERDICT — CONDITIONS FOR GENERAL AVAILABILITY AS A STANDALONE PRODUCT
OFFICEX Rent Roll is both the revenue core of the OFFICEX platform and a product that any landlord, property management company, FM company, managed service provider or flex operator can buy on its own.
